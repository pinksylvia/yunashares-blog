# Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** April 2026

Welcome to Yuna Shares (yunashares.blog). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect information when you visit our website. By using this site, you agree to the practices described below.

If you are looking for the Traditional Chinese version, see [隱私權政策](/privacy/).

## Who We Are

Yuna Shares is a personal blog operated by Yuna, based in Taiwan. The site publishes content about parenting, travel, online shopping, and product reviews. Some content includes affiliate links — see our [Affiliate Disclosure](/en/affiliate-disclosure/) for details.

## Information We Collect

### 1. Information you provide voluntarily
- Name and email address when you contact us through the contact form
- Any message content you choose to send

### 2. Information collected automatically
- **Browser and device data**: browser type, operating system, screen resolution, language preference
- **Usage data**: pages viewed, time spent, referring URL, navigation paths
- **Approximate location**: country / city level, derived from IP address (we do not store full IP addresses)
- **Cookies and similar technologies**: see Cookies section below

### 3. Affiliate tracking
When you click an affiliate link on our site, the destination platform (e.g. Amazon, Agoda, KKday, Shopee) places its own tracking cookie in your browser. This is how the merchant attributes a sale to us. We do not have access to your purchase details — only aggregated commission reports.

## How We Use Information

We use the information we collect to:
- Operate, maintain, and improve the website
- Understand which content is useful to readers
- Track the performance of affiliate referrals (in aggregate)
- Respond to messages sent through the contact form
- Comply with legal obligations

We do **not** sell your personal information to third parties.

## Cookies and Tracking Technologies

This site uses cookies and similar technologies for the following purposes:

| Type | Purpose | Provider |
|------|---------|----------|
| Analytics | Measure traffic and engagement | Google Analytics 4 |
| Affiliate tracking | Attribute outbound clicks to merchants | Amazon Associates, Agoda, KKday, Shopee, PartnerBoost, others |
| Ads measurement | Measure ad campaign performance | Google Ads, Taboola |
| Functional | Remember language and display preferences | First-party |

You can control cookies through your browser settings. Disabling cookies may affect site functionality but will not prevent you from reading content.

## Your Rights

### If you are in the European Economic Area (GDPR)
You have the right to:
- Access the personal data we hold about you
- Request correction of inaccurate data
- Request deletion of your data
- Object to or restrict processing
- Data portability
- Lodge a complaint with your local supervisory authority

### If you are a California resident (CCPA / CPRA)
You have the right to:
- Know what personal information is collected and how it is used
- Request deletion of personal information
- Opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information (we do not sell your data)
- Non-discrimination for exercising your rights

To exercise any of these rights, contact us via the [Contact](/en/contact/) page.

## Children's Privacy

This site is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can remove it.

## Data Retention

- Analytics data is retained according to Google's default retention settings (currently 14 months).
- Contact form messages are retained for up to 24 months unless you request earlier deletion.
- We do not store payment data — all transactions occur on the merchant's site.

## Security

We use industry-standard measures to protect data, including HTTPS encryption for all pages and minimum-access principles for any stored information. No system is 100% secure — please do not send sensitive financial information through this site.

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy occasionally. The "Last updated" date at the top reflects the most recent change. Material changes will be highlighted on the site.
