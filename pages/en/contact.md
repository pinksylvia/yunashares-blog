## When Reaching Out

To help me reply quickly, please include in your email:

- Your **brand or company name**
- The **specific collaboration type** you have in mind
- Your **budget range** (if applicable)
- **Timeline expectations** — when do you need this live?

I usually reply within **1–2 business days** (Asia / Taipei time, GMT+8).
