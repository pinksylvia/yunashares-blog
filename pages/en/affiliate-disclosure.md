# Affiliate Disclosure

**Last updated:** April 2026

This page explains how Yuna Shares (yunashares.blog) participates in affiliate marketing programs, in compliance with the U.S. Federal Trade Commission's *Guides Concerning the Use of Endorsements and Testimonials in Advertising* (16 CFR Part 255), as well as the operating agreements of each affiliate program we participate in.

## What Affiliate Links Are

Some links on this site are **affiliate links**. When you click an affiliate link and make a purchase on the merchant's site, we may earn a commission — at **no additional cost to you**. The price you pay is exactly the same as if you had visited the merchant directly.

We use affiliate revenue to cover the costs of running this site (hosting, domain, content production) and to support the time we spend researching and reviewing products.

## Amazon Associates Disclosure

> Yuna Shares is a participant in the Amazon Services LLC Associates Program, an affiliate advertising program designed to provide a means for sites to earn advertising fees by advertising and linking to Amazon.com. As an Amazon Associate we earn from qualifying purchases.

This applies to Amazon.com, Amazon.ca, Amazon.co.uk, Amazon.de, Amazon.co.jp, and other Amazon storefronts where we participate.

## Other Affiliate Programs We Participate In

In addition to Amazon Associates, we may earn commissions from the following programs:

- **Agoda Partner Program** — hotel and accommodation bookings
- **KKday Affiliate Program** — tours, tickets, and travel experiences
- **Shopee Affiliate Program** — products available on Shopee Taiwan
- **PartnerBoost** — various brand partnerships
- **Other independent merchant or network programs** disclosed within individual posts where applicable

Each program has its own commission structure, cookie window, and attribution rules. We do not have access to your personal purchase details — we only see aggregated commission reports.

## Our Editorial Standards

All product recommendations on this site reflect our genuine opinions based on personal use, research, or both. We follow these editorial principles:

1. **No pay-for-positive-review**: We do not accept payment in exchange for positive reviews. Affiliate commission is paid by the merchant only when a reader chooses to purchase, and does not influence our coverage.
2. **Honest assessments**: When a product has limitations or downsides, we say so — even if we are an affiliate partner.
3. **Real experience first**: Wherever possible, we recommend products we have personally used. When a recommendation is based on research rather than direct use, we say so explicitly in the post.
4. **No fake scarcity**: We do not invent fake "limited time" or "selling out fast" claims. Promotional periods are reported accurately based on the merchant's published terms.

## How to Identify Affiliate Links

Affiliate links on this site may take any of the following forms:
- A direct merchant URL (e.g. an `amazon.com/dp/...` link with our tracking ID `tag=yunashares-20`)
- A short or branded link (e.g. `amzn.to/...`, `s.shopee.tw/...`)
- A button labeled "View on [merchant]", "Check price", "Shop now", or similar

In all cases, clicking the link will take you to the merchant's site. The presence of an affiliate disclosure on every post — usually at the top or bottom of the article — is your signal that the post may contain affiliate links.

## Sponsored or Gifted Content

Where a post is **sponsored** (the brand paid us a flat fee in addition to or instead of commission) or where the product was **gifted** (we received the product free of charge for review), this will be clearly disclosed at the top of the relevant post.

## Your Trust Matters

We take FTC compliance and reader trust seriously. If you ever feel a piece of content was misleading or that a disclosure was unclear, please [contact us](/contact/) — we will review and correct it.
